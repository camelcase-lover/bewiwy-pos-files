Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Bemiwy POS collects minimal data required to operate your point of sale system. This includes your company name, business email, and transaction data generated within the software. We do not sell your data to third parties.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the POS services, sync your transactions across your devices, and provide customer support. All data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.

3. Local Storage

For the desktop and mobile applications, your data is stored locally on your device. If you enable cloud sync, your data is securely transferred to our servers using industry-standard encryption.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for crash reporting and analytics to improve the app. These services do not have access to your financial or customer data.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to export, modify, or delete your data at any time directly from the settings panel of the Bemiwy POS application.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the WhatsApp support link provided in the app.
